Summer Camp Leader

Role overview

During the summer of 2025, I worked as an activity leader at Global College Summer Camp, an english school summer camp for international students. My main duties consisted of assisting the teachers during english classes, running my own elective classes, and supervising the students during outings.

I went into this job with the goal of making as large of a positive impact as I could. My intention was to make the kids my highest priority, and to go out of my way to ensure they had a great experience of the summer camp, and of their time in Canada in general - all while following my supervisors instructions, of course. I did this by getting to know each of them personally - starting conversations and asking them questions, by doing so, I was trying to give them a feeling of security and belonging, which was especially meaningful for those who were away from home for the first time. By doing this, I believe I helped foster some community amongst the students.

Takeaways

By the end of the summer camp, I was clearly able to see that my efforts had an impact, as I several emotional goodbyes with many of the students. I saw how much they appreciated me, which was an incredibly rewarding feeling, and I understood firsthand how valuable feelings like trust and belonging are. This experience taught me that putting extra effort into something you are passionate about is always a worthy endeavor, and has made me a more motivated person as a result.

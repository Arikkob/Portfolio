Team Talons

About Team Talons

Team Talons is a weekly, afterschool sports program created for elementary school students to foster community and encourage healthy, physical activity. I founded Team Talons in September 2024 alongside five other students as part of the Con X leadership program at Gleneagle Secondary School.

The program was successfully run at three elementary school over the course of the 2024-2025 school year, with over 150+ kids participating in total.

My Role

As this was a brand new program, a sizeable part of my work was pitching our program to various elementary schools via email. Once we had established the program at a few Elementary Schools, my responsibilities consisted of coordinating with the school admin and parents of the students, collecting sponsorships, and gathering volunteers.

As for the program itself, I helped create activity plans that were fun and engaging for the kids. I led and supervised the activities, oftentimes participating in the myself, and brought as much positivity and energy as I could. An essential part of running the program succesfully was maintaining the kids' well-being, it required a lot of conflict resolution, active listening, and adapting to their needs.

Skills developed

This experience really trained my empathy, communication, and conflict resolution skills, as younger children can be very unpredictable, and it was important to actively understand their feelings to ensure they have a good time. Because the mood is often based upon the attitude of the leader, the role required me to bring constant high, positive energy, which in turn really grew my confidence as a leader. I was a rather timid person before this program, and the courage I gained from constantly putting myself outside of my comfort zone in Team Talons has made a huge impact on other parts of my life.

Overall, I had lots of fun and became good friends with some of the kids. This experience taught me that bringing joy to others is truly one of the best things in the world.

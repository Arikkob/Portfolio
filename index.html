A Bit More About Me

Background

I was born in Israel, where I lived for 7 years before moving to the Vancouver area, which is where I've lived since with my parents, two younger sisters, my cat and my dog.
Since childhood, I've always had a huge appreciation for math and logic, which snowballed into my passion for science and engineering. After doing a high school science project on radio waves, I became particularly fascinated by electricity & magnetism, and all the incredible ways humanity has harnessed these phenomena to make truly baffling technology.
Most of all, I love the process of creative problem solving, finding a clever solution to a problem remains simply the most satisfying thing in the world.

Hobbies and Passions

Besides engineering, there is a lot that brings me joy in life.
Something that has always consistently done that is soccer. I've been playing club soccer since I was four years old, and it remains as one of my favourite activities.
Another major passion of mine is music, both listening and playing instruments, namely the piano and guitar. I love music and the emotions it can induce in, and I someday want to seriously try making music myself.
A new but thriving hobby of mine is learning languages. In addition to english I can speak Russian (Native), decent French, and I used to speak Hebrew but forgot most of it. I have currently been self-learning korean for the past six months, which has been going very well, and I hope to try my hand at Mandarin Chinese, Hindi, and Farsi in the future.
That said, my greatest passion is human connection, which is perhaps the reason why I love working in teams and taking on leadership initiatives.
